Demo mode: devices and apps are simulated.

Output

Choose the playback side of a virtual cable (CABLE Input). Other apps use its recording side (CABLE Output) as their mic.

To hear the mix yourself, use Listen on Master.

Mixer

  • Each card is a source. Master sets the level of the whole mix.
  • Click a dB value to type one. Double-click a slider to reset it.
  • Drag a card's handle to reorder.
  • A mic card's settings ⚙ have noise suppression, automatic level and the noise gate.
  • Listen on Master plays the mix on your default output.

Soundboard

  • Click a sound to play it, and again to restart it.
  • Add MP3, WAV, OGG, FLAC or M4A files up to 60 seconds, or drop them here.
  • In a sound's settings ⚙, rename, trim or delete it, and set its volume. Drag to reorder.
  • Hear sounds plays them on your default output too.

Music

  • Plays files and folders into the mix. Files aren't copied.
  • Click a track to play it, or its × to remove it from the playlist.
  • Hear music plays it on your default output too.

            Getting started

            1. Install a virtual cable, such as the free VB-Audio Virtual Cable, then restart your PC.
            2. Output: choose CABLE Input. It's picked for you if the cable is installed.
            3. Sources: your default mic is already added.
              • Apps and other inputs: Add source.
              • Sound effects: Add sounds.
              • A playlist: Add music.
            4. In the app you talk in, choose CABLE Output as the mic. See Using the mic in other apps.

            Tip: Setup check in the top bar finds problems with any of this, and fixes most of them.

            Using the mic in other apps

            • Microphone: choose CABLE Output (VB-Audio Virtual Cable).
            • Windows default output: keep it on your speakers or headphones, not the cable.
            • Turn off the app's own mic processing. Its noise suppression and automatic sensitivity cut out sounds and music. Use this mixer's mic settings instead.

            Discord

            Settings → Voice & Video:

            • Input Device: CABLE Output.
            • Input Sensitivity: automatic off, slider all the way left.
            • Noise Suppression: None.
            • Echo Cancellation and Automatic Gain Control: off.

            Too quiet now? Turn on Automatic level for your mic.

            OBS

            • Add an Audio Input Capture source with CABLE Output.
            • Remove any source that records your mic directly, or your voice is in twice.

            Teams, Zoom and others

            • Choose CABLE Output as the microphone, and turn off noise suppression.

            Sources and the mixer

            • Apps capture everything an app plays, including all of a browser's tabs.
              • The app still plays on your speakers too.
              • Matched by name, so capture resumes when the app restarts.
              • Windows can't capture a single tab or window.
            • Mics and inputs: a device, or Default microphone, which follows Windows. The cable's own recording side isn't allowed: it would loop the mix into itself.
              • Others hear it; you don't, unless you turn on Hear yourself in its settings ⚙.
              • Mute stops it going to others. Hear yourself still plays it to you.
            • Master sets the level of the whole mix. A limiter stops it clipping.
            • Listen plays the mix on your default output, so you hear what others hear.
              • Use headphones.
              • It turns off when the last page using it closes.

            Mic settings

            Open them with a mic card's settings ⚙. They only affect that mic.

            • Hear yourself: plays the mic to you too, on your default output, about 50 ms late.
              • Use headphones, or it can feed back.
              • Off while Listen is on, which already plays you the whole mix.
            • Echo cancellation: for speakers. Stops others hearing themselves, or your game, through your mic.
              • Removes what plays on your Windows default output from your mic. That must be your speakers, not the cable.
              • Not needed with headphones.
              • Delays your mic by 9 ms. The other sources are delayed to match.
            • Noise suppression: removes steady noise such as fans and hum.
            • Automatic level: keeps your voice at a steady level.
              • Adjusts slowly, and only while you talk.
              • Shows how much it's adjusting, and starts from there next time.
            • Noise gate: silences your mic between words.
              • When it hears your voice: opens for speech louder than the room.
              • Above a level I set: opens when the meter passes your line. Set the line just above the meter while you're quiet.
              • Stays open for 200 ms after you stop, so word endings aren't cut.
            • Extra strength (automatic gate only): ignores knocks and clicks.
              • Delays your mic by 30 ms.
              • Keep everything else in time delays the other sources to match, e.g. to sing along to music.

            Soundboard

            • Add: MP3, WAV, OGG, FLAC or M4A, with Add sounds or by dropping files.
              • Up to 60 seconds and 20 MB each, 64 sounds.
            • Play: click a sound. Click again to restart it. Stop all stops everything.
            • Edit: in a sound's settings ⚙, rename, trim or delete it, and set its volume.
              • Drag the waveform's edges or type times. Trim silence cuts quiet ends.
              • Preview plays it only to you.
              • The file isn't changed: Reset undoes the trim.
            • Reorder: drag, or focus a sound and press Alt+← or Alt+→.
            • Even out volumes (Settings, on by default): turns each sound up or down to a similar loudness, without distorting it.
            • Hear sounds: plays the soundboard on your default output at Your volume. Paused while Listen is on.

            Note: the soundboard is also a card in the mixer, with its own volume, mute and hotkeys.

            Music

            • Add: folders (with subfolders) or files, by path or with Folders… / Files…. Files aren't copied.
            • Play: click a track. Shuffle and repeat are by the play button.
            • Remove: a track's × takes it out of the playlist. Restore removed tracks, in Add music, brings it back. Clear empties the playlist.
            • Turn the music down while I talk: lowers it while your mic hears you.
            • Hear music: plays it on your default output at Your volume. Paused while Listen is on.

            Note: no files are ever deleted. The music is also a card in the mixer, with its own volume, mute and hotkeys.

            Hotkeys

            • Work anywhere, including in games.
            • Actions: toggle mute, push to talk, push to mute, play sound, stop all sounds, and music play/pause, next and previous.
            • Change keys: click a hotkey's keys and press new ones. Mouse side buttons (Mouse4, Mouse5) work too.

            Hotkeys don't work in some games

            • Why: Windows hides keys typed into apps running as administrator (some games and launchers).
            • Fix: turn on Settings → Start with Windows → as Administrator, or right-click virtual-audio-mixer.exe → Run as administrator.
              • Windows asks for permission once.
              • It runs a copy in Program Files, which updates itself.
            • Some anti-cheat software blocks hotkeys anyway.

            Troubleshooting

            • Others hear themselves or your game: use headphones, or turn on Echo cancellation in your mic's settings ⚙. It needs your speakers as the Windows default output.
            • Others hear nothing: run Setup check. Check the app uses CABLE Output, and the mic card's meter moves when you talk.
            • Others say I'm quiet: turn on Automatic level in your mic's settings ⚙. Also ask them to check your User Volume in Discord (right-click your name).
            • Everything I play ends up in the mic: your Windows default output is the cable. Set it back to your speakers or headphones.
            • Listen, Hear sounds or Hear yourself won't play: your default output is the device the mix goes to.
            • The gate cuts out quiet words: use Above a level I set and lower the line.
            • Hotkeys don't work in a game: see Hotkeys.

            Files

            All in %APPDATA%\VirtualAudioMixer:

            • config.json: your settings. One that can't be read is moved aside as config.<time>.json.bad.
            • sounds\: your soundboard sounds.
            • log.txt and log.previous.txt: this run's log and the last one. The tray's Open log file opens it.

            Updates

            • Settings → Updates sets how they work. Check now looks straight away.
            • Restart to update, in the bar at the top, installs a downloaded update. Otherwise it's installed the next time the mixer starts.
            • Safe: only genuine, signed releases are installed. A check only asks GitHub for the latest version number.
            • The copy that starts as administrator updates itself too.
            • Test builds don't update themselves. Install the latest release, in Settings → Updates, swaps one for the latest release.

            Removing it

            1. Turn off Start with Windows in Settings. This removes what starts it when you sign in, and the copies it installed.
            2. Quit from the tray icon.
            3. Delete the exe you downloaded, and these folders if they're still there:
              • %APPDATA%\VirtualAudioMixer: your settings, sounds and logs.
              • %LOCALAPPDATA%\Programs\Virtual Audio Mixer
              • C:\Program Files\Virtual Audio Mixer (it goes by itself when you restart)
            4. VB-Audio Virtual Cable, if you want it gone too: run its setup again, choose Remove Driver, and restart.
